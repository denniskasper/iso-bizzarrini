# A Sample is the one public part of the archive, and shows only what the factory printed

ADR 0006 keeps every Scan off public paths because members contribute "on the understanding that
only paid-up members can see the archive". The Club wants Visitors to see what membership gives,
and Google to find it (issue #99). So an Administrator may mark a Published Tavola as a **Sample**,
and that Tavola is shown to Visitors and may be indexed.

The promise is kept by drawing the line at **what the factory printed**. A Sample shows its
drawing, its Marks and, at the one Position an Administrator opened, the Parts listed there:
Catalog Number, the Italian and English names, quantity and Catalog Note. It never shows what the
club added: no Contribution, Note, Photograph or Specification, and none of the club's
cross-referencing (Fitment, Equivalents, Related Positions), on the Sample or on the Parts at its
open Position. The other numbers are drawn and locked. A Sample is at its Tavola's own address, so
a signed-in member following the same link sees the whole Tavola.

Beyond the Samples, a Visitor sees **which Models the archive covers and how many Published Tavole
each has**: the depth, and none of the content. Every Tavola that is not a Sample stays exactly as
private as ADR 0006 describes, its name included, and whatever else a Visitor asks for is refused
as it is today, with the way to join.

**A Sample's drawing is a smaller public copy, not its Scan.** It is sharp enough to read the
numbers and not to print the sheet, made when the Tavola is marked and deleted when it is
unmarked. The Scan itself stays behind the authenticated route of ADR 0013, so marking a Sample
never makes the archive's own image public.

**Only the Visitor pages are indexed**: the landing page and the Samples. Everything else keeps
`noindex`, and a non-production system keeps it everywhere (ADR 0012).

## Considered options

- **No public part at all.** Keeps ADR 0006 to the letter, but leaves a Visitor with "The archive
  is members-only" and nothing to judge membership by. The Club asked for the Sample.
- **The full Scan, public.** Simpler, but an indexed full-resolution sheet is the archive's own
  material given away, and one download serves anybody who wanted only that sheet.
- **A fixed number of Samples, enforced.** The Club chose five; a cap in the code would only need
  changing. Which Tavole, and how many, is the Administrators' judgment.
