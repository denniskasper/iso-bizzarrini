# Ricambi's accent is its own pair, not the Club's teal

Ricambi's surfaces, rules, text and body face are still the Club's, measured from its site. Its
accent is not. The Club's one teal is replaced by a pair with separate jobs: **champagne** for
what is pressed, **ice blue** for what points at something — a Mark on a drawing, the Position
it belongs to, a link. A failure gets a red of its own that means nothing else, and the focus
ring becomes ink rather than amber, which sat too close to champagne. Display lettering moves from Raleway to a tall condensed
capital, which is closer to the Club's own lockup than Raleway was.

## Why

The first design made the archive and the Club site share one accent so the two would read as
one organisation. Seen built, the owner did not like it. A prototype put five structurally
different designs and twelve candidate accents on graphite side by side; the verdict was the
"Showroom" design with this pair. The prototype is kept on the `prototype/ricambi-design` branch
as the primary source.

Two colours rather than one because the old single accent was doing two unrelated jobs — "press
this" and "this is the thing on the drawing" — and on a screen that has both, a member had to
work out which was meant.

## What it costs

- The two properties no longer share an accent. What still ties them is the graphite, the body
  face, the roundel and the lettering of the wordmark.
- "One accent" was a rule that was easy to police. "Two, with separate jobs" is not: champagne
  on anything that is not the dominant action, or ice blue on decoration, is the drift to watch.
- Champagne is pale, so it only works as a fill on graphite. On paper both colours are taken
  from the deep end of their hues, and a Mark, which only ever sits on paper, is always the deep
  blue. The reasoning and the measured ratios are in `docs/design/visual-language.md`.

## Not decided here

The prototype drew its sample drawing reversed, white line on graphite. Real scans are black on
white paper and stay that way; whether to reverse them in the dark theme is a separate decision
that needs real scans to judge.
