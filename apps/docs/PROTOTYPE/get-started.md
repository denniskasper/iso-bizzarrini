Ricambi works the way you would use the factory's parts book on the bench: you **choose the car**, **open the drawing**, **find the number** and **read the part**. This page walks that once, start to finish. It takes about ten minutes, and works the same on a phone as on a computer.

You need to be signed in. If the Club has just added you, the e-mail it sent has a link to choose your password. See *Signing in and your password*.

## 1. Choose the car

Ricambi opens on **Covered cars**: every car in the archive, grouped by brand, with its series and years.

> 📷 *Covered cars, at phone width (Integration)*

Press the car you are working on. If you are holding a part and don't know which car it belongs to, use **Or start from a number stamped on the part** instead. See *Start from a number stamped on the part*.

## 2. Open the drawing

A car's page shows its factory drawings as a sheet of small pictures, each with its number and its name in Italian and in English. The factory catalog calls each drawing a **Tavola**, and so does Ricambi: *Tavola 51* is drawing 51 of that car's catalog. Another car's Tavola 51 is a different drawing.

> 📷 *A car's drawings, filtered to one system (Integration)*

To narrow the list, press one of the systems above the pictures, such as **Brakes**, **Electrical** or **Fuel**. Then press the drawing you want.

Some English names carry a small **Machine translation** tag. That name was translated by machine from the factory's Italian, and nobody at the Club has checked it yet. The Italian next to it is what the factory printed.

## 3. Find the number

The drawing fills the top of the page. Move around it the way you would a photograph:

- **Drag** to move it, and **pinch** or scroll to zoom.
- **Double-tap** to zoom in. At full zoom, a double tap takes you back to the whole drawing.
- **Full screen** gives the drawing the whole screen, which helps on a phone.

Every number the factory printed is circled. Press the one you want.

If the number is hard to find on the drawing, type it into **Go to a number** beside the drawing instead.

> 📷 *A Tavola zoomed in, a circle pressed, its card open (phone width, Integration)*

## 4. Read the part

Pressing a circle opens the Position: everything the archive knows about that number on this drawing. On a phone it appears below the drawing. On a wider screen it sits beside it.

> 📷 *The reading panel for one Position (Integration)*

From the top, it tells you:

- **The part at this position**: the part the number points at, with how many are fitted per car (**×2 per car**) and its **Catalog number**, the number the factory ordered it by. Where the factory's drawing office recorded its own drawing number, the **Disegno origine** appears as well.
- **Alternatives**: when more than one part is drawn at a number. Either the factory offered a choice, or it is a left and right pair.
- **Also needed**: the screws, washers and clips that fasten the part. They share its number but aren't drawn. Order them along with it.
- **From the factory catalog**: notes the factory printed, in its own words.
- **The club's note**: what the Club's Editors have found out since.
- **Fits**, **Instead of the original** and **Seen for sale**: which cars the part fits, other ways to get it, and where it has been offered, each price with the date it was seen.

**Nothing is recorded at this position yet** means the factory printed the number, but nobody has entered the part there.

To send somebody this exact place, press **Copy link**. The link opens the same drawing at the same number, for any member.

## 5. Check against the factory's own sheet

Below the parts are **The factory's table sheets**: pictures of the pages the parts list was read from. Press **Sheet 1** to open it in the same viewer as the drawing. Compare it with what Ricambi says whenever a quantity or a number matters.

## When you know something the archive doesn't

Below a part, press **Add what you know about this part** (or **Report something wrong**). Write it in your own words and add photographs if you have them. **Take a photograph** opens your phone's camera. Then press **Send to the Editors**.

Nobody else sees it until an Editor has read it. After that, it is published under your name. See *Tell the Club what you know*.
