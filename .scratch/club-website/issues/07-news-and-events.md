# 07 — News and Events, editable

**What to build:** A Club editor adds a news item or an event, with pictures, and it appears in
the listing and on its own page. This is the content that actually changes through the year, so
it is the real test of whether the CMS works for the people who have to use it.

**Blocked by:** 06 — Sveltia CMS editing a real page end to end.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] News and Events modelled as content collections with typed frontmatter
- [ ] Listing pages and individual item pages for both, in the site's existing structure
- [ ] Events carry their date and sort sensibly; past events remain reachable
- [ ] Both are editable in the CMS as their own collections
- [ ] Images can be uploaded from the CMS and placed in an item
- [ ] Uploaded images are converted to a modern format on upload and optimised at build; no unoptimised original is shipped
- [ ] A previously uploaded image can be found and reused rather than uploaded again
- [ ] Existing news and event items from the old site are carried across
- [ ] UI and design work uses the `/frontend-design` skill
