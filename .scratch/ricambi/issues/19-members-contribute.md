# 19 — Members contribute

**What to build:** A member adds what they know — to a Part, or to an empty Position — with
photographs if they have them, and can see that it arrived and is waiting to be checked.

Targeting a **Position** matters as much as targeting a Part. The archive deliberately shows
members which circles have nothing recorded; if the only thing you can annotate is a Part that
does not exist yet, the system points at a gap and then offers no way to fill it.

**Blocked by:** 10 — Part photographs; 15 — Authorisation and the renewal screen.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A member writes a Contribution against a **Part or a Position**, in free text
- [ ] Photographs can be attached, reusing the private upload and serving path from ticket 10
- [ ] A photograph can be **taken with the device camera** from within the form, not only picked from a gallery. The member is standing at the car holding the part; making them leave, photograph, and come back loses the contribution
- [ ] A Contribution records its author and the date it was written
- [ ] After sending, the member sees their own Contribution and that it is **waiting to be checked**
- [ ] Nobody else sees it until it is published — not other members, not search
- [ ] A member can report that a Part record is wrong, through the same mechanism
- [ ] Published Contributions display with their author's name and date
- [ ] A member can read others' published Contributions on a Part
- [ ] Submitting is possible on a phone, since much of this gets written in a garage
- [ ] There is no field-level diff or proposed-edit interface — deliberately; corrections reach the record through an Editor
- [ ] UI and design work uses the `/frontend-design` skill
